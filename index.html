Drop-Off

Scheduled home drop-off

Most appointments are completed as scheduled drop-offs. To protect family privacy and keep things organized, the exact drop-off location is provided only after the booking is confirmed.

  • Arrival: Please arrive at your confirmed time.
  • Completion: We provide an estimated pickup window.
  • Changes: If the vehicle needs significantly more work, we contact you before continuing.
Mobile Service

Available through trusted referrals

Mobile detailing is offered selectively to people we already know, returning customers, friends and family, or approved referrals. This keeps the service comfortable, safe and personal for everyone involved.

  • Returning customers: Ask when booking.
  • Friends & family: Mobile may be available.
  • Approved referrals: We’ll confirm before scheduling.
Who We Are

Young entrepreneurs. Real work. Family supported.

BB Detailing started as a way for young entrepreneurs to learn how to build something of their own. That means showing up, communicating clearly, taking pride in the details, and earning every customer’s trust through the work.

Family support is there behind the scenes, but the goal is simple: give customers a great clean at a fair price while building confidence, responsibility and real-world business experience.

Our standard: honest work, respectful service, fair value — and cars that leave looking a whole lot better than when they arrived.
